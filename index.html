Multi-Representative Sales Intelligence Platform — 6 Reps, 8,233 Clients

Challenge: A sales organization with 6 representatives and 8,233+ client records had no unified view of performance gaps, contact aging, or comparative rep efficiency.

Designed and delivered a full-featured multi-tab interactive web dashboard covering the entire sales team. The platform features four distinct analytical modules: (1) Sales Overview with filterable KPIs and client status distribution donut charts; (2) Contact Activity with per-rep contact rate breakdowns and stacked comparison charts; (3) Gap Analysis with automated priority flags identifying reps with critical outreach deficits — Sameh Ali flagged at 59% not-contacted (1,487 clients); and (4) Aging & Timeline showing month-by-month contact history and time-since-last-contact distribution for each rep. The dashboard includes dynamic dropdowns for rep and status filtering, with all charts updating in real time.

Rep-Level Contact Rate Audit & Activity Intelligence Report

Challenge: Management could not identify which reps were falling behind on client outreach or quantify the revenue risk of uncontacted prospects.

Delivered a granular contact activity analysis module identifying significant performance disparities across the team. Ahmed Magdy led the team with a 96% contact rate (2,052 clients contacted), while Sameh Ali and Hassan Nasser showed critical gaps at 41% and 49% respectively — representing over 2,500 high-risk uncontacted clients. The analysis included aging breakdowns (this week / this month / 1–3 months / 3–6 months / 6+ months), a 12-month contact trend line, and a per-rep priority matrix that automatically flagged "Medium Priority" escalation for accounts requiring immediate outreach. The resulting intelligence directly informed resource reallocation and outreach prioritization decisions.
